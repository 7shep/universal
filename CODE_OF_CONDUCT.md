# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Universal welcoming and safe for everyone, regardless
of experience level, identity, background, appearance, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and code rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and contributors who are still learning the project.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy and remove secrets or personal information from public reports.
- Honor maintainers' decisions about project scope, while disagreeing professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Abusing project channels for spam or unrelated promotion.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community.

When deciding on an action, maintainers will consider the behavior's impact, context, repetition,
and the participant's response to feedback. Enforcement decisions may be explained publicly when
doing so does not compromise anyone's privacy or safety.

## Reporting

Report project conduct concerns privately to
[shepedits@gmail.com](mailto:shepedits@gmail.com). For urgent platform-safety concerns, use
[GitHub's Report Abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not use a public
issue when doing so would expose sensitive or personal information.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Maintainers will protect the
reporter's privacy and safety to the extent possible and will disclose information only as needed
to investigate and respond.
